# Main playlist

## Name (paste)

**Small Business Breakdowns**

## Description (paste)

```
Vending routes, car washes, laundromats, funeral homes: what it costs to buy one, what the owner really keeps, and what it's worth when you sell. Real contracts, real listings, real numbers. Not financial advice.
```

## Why

- **Playlists are usually named like a show or a shelf label:** 2–4 plain words, used the same way for every episode ([Timeskip](https://timeskip.io/blog/playlist-organization), [Google podcast playlist guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/podcast_playlist_guide.pdf)). It isn't a sentence.
- **"Breakdowns" covers buying, owning and selling,** not just earnings, and "small business" is the phrase people search.
- **Playlists can show up in YouTube search,** so the description names the businesses and the three stages people search for ([Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/hub/youtube/playlists)).
- **Once there are 4–7 episodes,** add focused shelves such as "Buying a Business" and "Selling Your Business" ([Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/hub/youtube/playlists)).
