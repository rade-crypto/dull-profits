# Main playlist

## Name (paste)

**How Much Small Businesses Really Make**

## Description (paste, 365 characters)

```
Vending machines, car washes, laundromats, funeral homes: what these small businesses really make, and how much the owner keeps after the supplier, the landlord, the payment companies and the running costs. Every episode follows the money with real contracts, sale listings and operator numbers, so you know what you're buying before you buy it. Not financial advice.
```

## Why

- **Playlists rank in YouTube search on their own,** so the name should be a phrase people type, not a slogan ([Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/hub/youtube/playlists)). "How much does a … make" is how people search every one of these businesses: "how much does a vending machine make" is Google's first suggestion, and car washes and laundromats work the same way (see `research/ep1-packaging/reddit-web.md`).
- **Specific beats generic.** A bare keyword like "Small Business" is a common mistake. One clear keyword in a readable title works better ([Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/boosting-your-youtube-views-and-retention-with-playlists/), [Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/hub/youtube/playlists)).
- **The description names each business,** so the playlist can match those searches too ([Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/boosting-your-youtube-views-and-retention-with-playlists/)). It also says why to watch (real documents) in the channel's calm tone, with no hype.
- **Left out on purpose:** "boring" (titles with it did 0.5× in our outlier study) and "passive income".
- **When there are 4–7 episodes,** split them into focused playlists, for example "Buying a Small Business" and "Selling Your Business" ([Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/hub/youtube/playlists)).
