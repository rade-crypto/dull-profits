# Episode 2, car washes: decision report

*Nov 27, 2026. The research is in `research/ep2-car-washes/` (4 files, ~13,700 words, every figure linked).*

## 1. The angle
**"Thirty dollars a month, unlimited washes. Follow it, and see who really gets paid. Then look at why the biggest owners are selling, writing it off or going bankrupt."**

- Episode 1 promised: *"what one wash really costs, who gets paid, and why big investors keep buying them."* This delivers that, plus a twist the research turned up. **The buying spree has turned:**
  - Mister Car Wash went public at **$15** a share (2021). Its private-equity owner took it back private at **$7** (May 2026).
  - Zips went bankrupt with **$653.9M** of debt (Feb 2025).
  - Driven Brands wrote off **$1.18B** on its car washes, then sold 382 sites for **$385M**.
- **Why this angle:** competitors either count cash on camera (Stephen the Investor, up to 7.7M views) or make 7-minute "Wall Street loves car washes" pieces dated before the turn (Bloomberg Originals, 329K). About 50 faceless "economics of a car wash" videos sit under 1K views. **No one follows one membership to the landlord, the franchisor, the lender and private equity, with the filings on screen.**
- **For our 40–65 viewers who might buy or sell:**
  - The buyer's math: a $700K broker-sold wash, 10% down, an SBA loan.
  - The seller's question: who pays what. Private equity paid about 9× earnings; a small wash sells for about 4.7×.

## 2. The spine: The Split of a $30 membership
Mister's members paid **$30.38 a month** on average in 2025 (from its 10-K). The costs are what **mature Tommy's Express franchise sites** reported in the company's franchise disclosure. Rent is the average for car washes listed for sale (Sept 2026).

| Slice | Of $30.00 | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Workers | $6.84 | new neutral |
| Chemicals, water and power | $4.47 | supplier blue |
| Repairs and equipment upkeep | $3.06 | supplier blue (lighter) |
| The landlord (rent, property tax, insurance) | $5.28 | location teal |
| The brand (royalty) and office costs | $3.41 | new neutral |
| Card fees and software | $0.66 | payments ochre |
| **Owner, before income tax and loan payments** | **$6.28** | white |

The slices add up exactly. Sales tax is $0 in the base case (many states don't tax car washes; Wisconsin does, at 5% and more).

## 5. Things the script avoids, and why
- **No "passive income", no cash piles, no "boring"** (channel rules and title data).
- **No cars-per-day figure.** No primary source publishes one, so the script uses sales per site instead.
- **No "car washes default more" claim.** Our own count of SBA loans shows the opposite, and the script says so to stay fair.
- **The narrator never says "I" or claims to own a wash, and gives no personal advice** (YouTube's 2026 rule on AI hosts).
