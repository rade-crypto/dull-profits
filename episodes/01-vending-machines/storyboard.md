# Episode 1 · Storyboard: "The Two-Dollar Soda" (DRAFT v0, before the competitor research)

> **This is a draft.** It's built from the fact-checked script, your voiceover's timings and the earlier visual research.
> The competitor and comment research is still running. When it's done I'll revise this storyboard, and every change will be marked.

**Length:** 11:19, timed to your ElevenLabs voiceover ("Bill"). I cut out the two places where it read the part headings aloud.

**Key**
- 🎥 Footage, full screen.
- 📊 One of our graphics over darkened footage.
- 🟫 Full-screen graphic.
- 📄 Deal File: a real source document with a highlighter sweep.
- ⏳ Clip picked but not downloaded yet. Magnific's download limit paused us; it resets every ~15 minutes.

**What it's based on**
- **Script:** `script-v2.md`, fact-checked against primary sources (22 changes; see `fact-check.md`).
- **Look:** `docs/visual-style-guide.md`, from the earlier research on 11 business-explainer channels and on how older viewers watch on TVs:
  - every number appears on screen as it's spoken;
  - key numbers are 120–200 px, nothing smaller than 36 px;
  - the bottom 15% stays clear;
  - one idea per shot, 4–8 seconds each;
  - calm cuts, with no whooshes or zoom-punches.
- **The brand's two recurring devices:**
  - **The Split**, the $2.00 bar that slices into who gets paid. It opens, explains and closes the episode.
  - **The Deal File**, which shows the real contract or report each number comes from.
- **Screen mix:** about 60% footage, 25% graphics over footage, 15% documents.
- **Competition and differentiator:** that research is still running. This doc gets a section on it when it lands, and any storyboard changes will be marked.
