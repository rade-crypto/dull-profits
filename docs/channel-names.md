## Not checked from here
Gmail, Instagram, TikTok and X can't be reliably checked from this machine. To check them:
1. Search the name on **namechk.com** (checks most socials at once).
2. Try it on Gmail's signup page (needs 6+ characters; Gmail ignores dots).

## Setup tips
- Create the Gmail first, then make the YouTube channel as a **Brand Account**. That keeps it separate from your personal account and lets you add editors later.
- A domain costs about $10–12 a year (Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun sell at cost). It's the only cost here, and cheap insurance even if you don't build a site yet.
- Before you commit, search the name on the US trademark database: tmsearch.uspto.gov.
