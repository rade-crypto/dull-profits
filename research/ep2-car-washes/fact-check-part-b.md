# Episode 2 fact-check, part B: "The private equity bet" to the end

Checked against `episodes/02-car-washes/script-v1.md` on 2026-11-27, by an independent checker who did not write the script.
Everything was checked against primary sources where they could be reached. The research notes were used only as leads.

**Blocked sites (not bypassed):** BizBuySell (HTTP 403), eCFR (bot check), leginfo.legislature.ca.gov and Justia (403).
For each one I used the best other source, named in its row. **BizBuySell's own page still needs a browser check before publishing.**

**Verdicts:** ✅ correct as written · ⚠️ correct but imprecise, overstated or with the wrong source tag · ❌ wrong, or the arithmetic doesn't work.

## Must fix before recording

1. **#28, BizBuySell multiple.** $700,000 ÷ $230,000 is 3.0×, not 4.7×. These are separate medians (32 sales) and can't be divided into one another.
2. **#33, the worked loan example.** It pairs the median price with an unrelated median cash flow. That makes the buyer's leftover look about $80,000 too high ($135K instead of about $55K).
3. **#29, "Notice the gap".** This compares a chain's EBITDA multiple with a small wash's SDE multiple, which isn't like for like. A broker also says single sites with land trade at 8–10× EBITDA.
4. **#23, the Q3 comparable-store sales bar.** It says 3.4%. The filing says 3.1%.
5. **#34, the visual colour.** The loan-payments slice is shown in location teal. Per the style guide it should be payments ochre `#B88E30`.
