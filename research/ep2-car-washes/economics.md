## 3. Follow the money: one $30 monthly membership

**Setup.** A mature express tunnel (5+ years). Costs from Tommy's Express 2024 actuals (FDD, P). Land and building leased at the average US car wash net-lease rent (B+E, S). Equipment upkeep reserve from Mister's maintenance capex (P). Price $30.00 a month, close to Mister's $30.38 realized average.

Rent share = $325,222 ÷ $2,204,358 = 14.75%. Equipment reserve = $28,529K ÷ $1,051,731K = 2.71%.

| Slice | % of sales | Of $30.00 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales tax | 0% here | **$0.00** | Revenue is net of sales tax (Mister 10-K). See note below |
| Workers (wages 19.43 + payroll tax 1.62 + workers comp 0.29 + health 0.80 + uniforms 0.11 + hiring 0.11 + training 0.44) | 22.80% | **$6.84** | Tommy's FDD, P |
| Chemicals | 5.75% | **$1.73** | Tommy's FDD, P |
| Water, sewer, power, gas, trash, phone | 9.13% | **$2.74** | Tommy's FDD, P |
| Repairs & maintenance | 7.49% | **$2.25** | Tommy's FDD, P |
| Card fees, bank, software | 2.19% | **$0.66** | Tommy's FDD, P |
| Rent (land + building) | 14.75% | **$4.43** | B+E Sept 2026 avg NOI ÷ Tommy's sales. Estimate (mixes two sources) |
| Property tax + insurance | 2.82% | **$0.85** | Tommy's FDD, P |
| Franchise royalty + brand fund | 5.00% | **$1.50** | Tommy's FDD, P (zero for an independent) |
| Marketing, admin, office, merchandise, permits, professional fees, transport, damage claims | 6.37% | **$1.91** | Tommy's FDD, P |
| Equipment replacement reserve | 2.71% | **$0.81** | Mister 10-K maintenance capex, P-derived |
| **Owner (before income tax and any loan on the equipment)** | 20.98% | **$6.28** | Remainder |
| **Total** | 100% | **$30.00** | Adds up exactly |

Sum check: 6.84 + 1.73 + 2.74 + 2.25 + 0.66 + 4.43 + 0.85 + 1.50 + 1.91 + 0.81 + 6.28 = 30.00.

Suggested grouping for The Split (five colours):
- **Tax** (rose): $0.00 sales tax here. Optionally show payroll + property tax: 1.62% + 1.37% = $0.90 (move out of Workers and Property).
- **Supplier** (blue): chemicals + utilities + repairs = $6.72.
- **Payments** (ochre): card fees and software = $0.66.
- **Location** (teal): rent + property tax + insurance = $5.28.
- **Owner** (white): $6.28.
- Workers ($6.84), brand/overhead ($3.41) and equipment reserve ($0.81) need their own slices or a neutral colour.

What's estimated or caveated:
- Tommy's costs are franchisee self-reported and unaudited.
- Rent is a market average applied to a different site. Many operators own the land; then the owner carries a mortgage instead.
- The $6.28 owner slice is before income tax. It is also before paying back the operator's own investment in equipment and fit-out (not in a real-estate lease). In the Tommy's model that is often $1.5M+ (estimate).
- Owner slice at site scale: 20.98% × $2.20M ≈ **$462K a year**, pre-tax, for a mature site (derived).
- Sales tax note: some states tax washes. Wisconsin taxes all car wash services, including coin-operated and automatic (5% state rate; counties add more) — P/H [WI DOR Fact Sheet 2108](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/DOR%20Publications/2108carwash.pdf). In Wisconsin, a $30 plan would cost the member at least $31.50, and the extra $1.50 goes to the state. Other states differ. Texas sources conflict; don't use Texas as the example.

Cross-check against Mister (P/H): Mister keeps 32.8% Adj. EBITDA after rent and HQ. Our single-site model keeps 21.0% after rent and capex reserve, and 23.7% before the reserve. Mister is higher because of scale, owned sites and no royalty. The two are consistent.

### Worked example: buying the median broker-sold car wash (estimate, all inputs sourced)
- Price $700,000. 10% down = $70,000 (SOP 50 10 8). Loan $630,000.
- At 8.75% (FY2026 median car wash rate) over 10 years: $94,700 a year. Over 25 years (with real estate): $62,200 a year.
- At the 10% cap: $99,900 (10 yr) or $68,700 (25 yr).
