The FDD's cost lines add up to 61.55%, which matches. The script's slices also sum to exactly $30.00, so its arithmetic holds. But the owner slice of 20.98% × $30 is **$6.30**, not $6.28. The $6.28 comes from rounding eleven sub-lines first. Rounding the seven groups gives **6.84 + 4.46 + 3.06 + 5.27 + 3.41 + 0.66 + 6.30 = 30.00**, and it matches "about 21 percent" and "$462K" exactly. Tally with those values: 30.00 → 23.16 → 18.70 → 15.64 → 10.37 → 6.96 → 6.30.
