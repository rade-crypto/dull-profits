### Patterns

- **One owner-creator dominates.** Stephen the Investor has 6 of the top 13. Car Wash Rob copies the formula (cash counts, "$3,000,000"). Both are self-serve sites shown from the owner's side.
- **$ figures:** 13 of the 25 titles contain a $ figure. They hold 9 of the top 11. The two big ones without $ are a Short and a "day in the life".
- **First person:** 10 of 25 ("I bought", "I spent", "my car wash"). All need a real owner on camera.
- **Questions:** only 3 of 25 use "?". The top one is a Short.
- **Warnings:** only 3 (Franchise Inspectors 81K, Every Level HQ 28K, Breaking Bad math 236K). Far fewer than in vending.
- **Private equity:** one big video (Bloomberg, 329K, 2 years old, 7 minutes). Everything since is under 15K.
- **Faceless that worked:** Breaking Blacklight (236K) and All Time (150K) both lead with a familiar hook (Breaking Bad, "conspiracy"), not with "economics".
- **Search phrases (Google/YouTube autocomplete, US, today):** "car wash for sale", "car wash business", "is a car wash a good investment (reddit)", "is buying a car wash a good investment", "how much does a car wash make a year / a month", "how much does a self serve car wash make", "why are there so many car washes (being built / popping up everywhere / now)", "are car washes profitable", "are car washes a scam", "car wash membership worth it", "car wash subscription business model", "buying a car wash business reddit", "mister / zips / el car wash private equity". "Car wash money laundering" completions are almost all GTA Online and Breaking Bad.

### What no one has done (our gap)

1. **Follow one dollar through one site.** Nobody splits a $25 membership or a $5M tunnel into who gets paid: the lender, the landlord, the equipment maker, chemicals and labour, the PE fund, and what's left.
2. **The landlord.** Nobody covers the sale-leaseback: the chain sells the land and building to an investor at a ~5% cap rate and rents it back. Operators on Car Wash Forum talk about it constantly (quotes 21–23). Mister Car Wash leases almost all its sites.
3. **The equipment makers.** Some build washes, sell franchises and supply equipment. Nobody covers that. Operators wonder about it (quote 24).
4. **The oversupply, with numbers.** Express openings fell from 943 in 2022 to about 550 in 2025 ([MMCG](https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/the-us-car-wash-industry-in-2026-the-gold-rush-meets-its-ceiling), *needs verification*). Zips filed Chapter 11 in 2025 (*needs verification*). Mister Car Wash IPO'd at $15 in 2021 and was taken private at $7 by Leonard Green, its majority owner, in a $3.1B deal (Feb 2026, closed May 2026) ([Mister Car Wash IR](https://mistercarwash.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mister-car-wash-be-taken-private-leonard-green-partners-700), [Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/why-did-mister-car-wash-stock-surge-pre-market-today/cZR0pwER4v5)). Bloomberg's video predates all of it.
5. **The documents.** No one puts the 10-K, the franchise disclosure document (FDD), a sale-leaseback listing or a bankruptcy filing on screen. That is our Deal File.
6. **The 40–65 buyer's question.** "Should I buy one, sell to a roll-up, or buy the real estate under one?" Owner channels answer "I did it"; explainers answer "Wall Street likes it". No one answers it for someone with capital.

## 3. Title candidates

Rules from our own data: a real $ figure ×1.9; a question mark ×0.64; "boring" ×0.5; statements beat questions; short, with key words first; "Inside X" strong ([decision report](/home/user/dull-profits/docs/decision-report.md), [Ep 1 titles](/home/user/dull-profits/research/ep1-packaging/youtube-titles.md)).

**Figures (all need verification before publishing):**
- **$5 million** express tunnel: consultants put ground-up builds at roughly $5–7M with land; one franchisor's FDD shows ~$3.5–4.2M for its base model ([iRecruit](https://www.irecruit.co/guides/car-wash-construction-cost), [MMCG](https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/tunnel-car-wash-construction-cost-breakdown-and-why-a-feasibility-study-is-essential), [1851 Franchise](https://1851franchise.com/tommys-express-car-wash-franchise-costs-fees-profit-2732879)). An operator says "about 5 mil".
- **$25 a month**: chain plans run about $20–40 a month ([Tommy's Express](https://tommys-express.com/?p=22) from $19.99). $25 is a realistic mid-tier price; pick one real chain's price for the script.
- **$15 → $7**: Mister Car Wash IPO price and take-private price (sourced above).
- **"Loses a third"**: operators estimate 20–35% (quotes 16–17). Anecdotal; needs an industry source or softer wording.
- **150 cars a day**: MMCG's break-even benchmark is 120–180 cars a day for a leveraged new build ([MMCG](https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/the-us-car-wash-industry-in-2026-the-gold-rush-meets-its-ceiling)). Needs checking.
