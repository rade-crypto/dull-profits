# Episode 2 (car washes): outlier videos

Read Nov 27, 2026. Every view count below was read that day. Links are `youtu.be/<id>`.

## Method

- **Same method as the [competitor landscape](/home/user/dull-profits/research/competitor-landscape.md) (section "Method").**
  - Outlier multiple = views ÷ median views of the ~20 long-form uploads around it on the same channel (10 before, 10 after).
  - "Chan ×" = views ÷ the channel's recent median (last 30 long videos at least 3 weeks old). The landscape also required this to be 3× or more.
  - **Outlier** = 3× or more and 50K views or more. **Small-channel outlier** = 3× or more, under 50K.
- **Search:** 48 YouTube searches with yt-dlp (`ytsearch60` / `ytsearch40`): car wash private equity, why so many car washes, membership / unlimited / subscription, bubble, oversupply, Mister Car Wash (stock, take-private), Zips bankruptcy, sale-leaseback, real estate, express tunnel cost, self-serve, "is a car wash a good investment", franchise, plus adjacent PE roll-up searches (HVAC, plumbing, vets, gyms, How Money Works, Modern MBA, Wall Street Millennial). 1,342 unique videos.
- **Channels:** the "Videos" tab (long-form only, no Shorts) of 61 channels, up to 400 uploads each, read with `yt-dlp --flat-playlist -J`.
- **Limits.**
  - Channel-page view counts are rounded by YouTube (2–3 significant figures). Where search gave the exact count, I used it.
  - Ages are YouTube's rounded labels ("2 months ago"). Shown as "~2mo".
  - Watch pages returned HTTP 429 (rate limit). I did not retry or work around it. So no upload dates, descriptions or voice checks.
  - "Faceless" is judged from thumbnails (contact sheet) and the earlier landscape labels.
  - Three tiny channels with huge multiples on short videos (Making Billions, Autowash, The Business Broker King) may have run paid ads. I can't check that. Treat them as unconfirmed.

## 3. What the car-wash outliers have in common

1. **A real owner and a real number win by far.** 8 of the 17 confirmed outliers are first-person owner videos with a price or profit in the title ("$425,000", "$3,000,000", "$500,000"). They need a face and a site. We can't copy that.
2. **For faceless channels, one angle over-performs: the membership.** Three unrelated faceless channels hit with it in the last 3 months:
   - Follow The Money (76×, 182K).
   - Small Print Economy (41×).
   - Lived It (6.2K on a 20-subscriber channel).
   All three thumbnails put the monthly price ($29.99, $39) next to a small cost or "who gets the rest".
3. **The topic did it, not the formula.** Follow The Money uses "How X Makes Money (It's Not the Y)" for every upload. His other recent videos get 20–1,700 views. Only the car wash broke out. Mr. Finance's car-wash template got 0.5×.
4. **Same title, very different results.** Car Wash Rob posted near-identical "$3,000,000 … Collect Cash" titles. They range from 31K to 694K. The thumbnail and timing matter as much as the words.
5. **Generic private equity is "normal", not an outlier.** Bloomberg's car-wash PE video was 2.0× (0.7× its channel). PE videos on Wendover, Modern MBA and How Money Works sit at 1.0–1.5×. PE only over-performs as a **specific, skeptical story**: Hard Hat Economics' roll-up piece (8.7×), Capital Fox's "$15 to $7" (7.4× on 2K subs), and Macy Gilliam's first-person "sell to private equity" (~5.7×).
6. **"Why are they everywhere" is hot right now.** Tyler Cauble's video reached 51,903 in 2 days (17×). The audience's own complaint is a live hook.
7. **Warnings work when they're the rare one.** Franchise Inspectors' "6 reasons to NOT Buy a Car Wash" is 13× and 35× its channel.
8. **Questions are mixed.** Some outliers use "?" (Stephen 59×, Car Wash Chronicles 57×). These are owner channels. No faceless outlier used a question.
9. **Length doesn't decide it.** Faceless outliers run 11–19 minutes. Owner outliers run 20–44 minutes.

## 4. What this means for our 3 titles

**(1) "A $30 Car Wash Membership. The Owner Keeps $6.28."**
- Best match to the only faceless pattern that works on this topic (membership, the viewer's own monthly bill).
- Two real $ figures, a statement, no question. That matches our own rules.
- "$6.28" reads as a computed figure, not a round guess. Owner channels show a precise $ figure helps.
- Risk: Lived It and Small Print are already on this angle. Ours has to look better and carry the full split (landlord, PE, equipment).
- **Recommend as the lead title.** Thumbnail: the $30 card sliced by The Split, owner slice in white. Don't repeat $6.28 on it.

**(2) "Mister Car Wash Went Public at $15. It Sold for $7."**
- Capital Fox proved the "$15 to $7" hook (7.4× on 2K subscribers). But the ceiling is small: 13K views.
- Stock and PE stories underperform their channels at scale (Bloomberg 2.0×, generic PE ~1–1.5×).
- It speaks to people who follow the stock, not to the 40–65 buyer or member.
- **Use as the third A/B option, or as a chapter title and a Short.**

**(3) "Inside a $5 Million Car Wash: Who Actually Gets Paid"**
- "$ price of the site" is the strongest pattern overall ($425,000, $3,000,000, $9,000,000). But those are owners on camera.
- "Who gets paid" matches Lived It's "WHO GETS THE REST?" thumbnail.
- It promises the whole money trail, including the landlord. Only 42 Freeway touched the landlord (5K views).
- **Recommend as the second A/B option** next to (1).

**Test & Compare:** run (1) vs (3), with (2) as the third option. All three keep "car wash" in the first 30 characters.
