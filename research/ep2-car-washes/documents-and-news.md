### DF5. SBA 7(a) loan data, car washes (NAICS 811192). PRIMARY dataset, our own calculation, high confidence
Source: SBA FOIA 7(a) files, as of Sep 30, 2026. Dataset page: https://data.sba.gov/dataset/7a-504-foia
Files: `FOIA_7a_FY2010_FY2019_asof_260930.csv`, `FOIA_7a_FY2020_Present_asof_260930.csv`
- Car wash 7(a) approvals peaked in **FY2021: 391 loans, $676M**. FY2025: 243 loans, $272M. FY2026: 192 loans, $208M. (Lender pullback / demand drop.)
- FY2010–2019 cohort, resolved loans: car washes **5.9%** charged off (111 of 1,879) vs **8.0%** for all industries.
- FY2020+ resolved so far: car washes 5.2% vs 9.9% all industries (young cohorts; most loans still open).
- Honest read: SBA car wash loans have *not* defaulted more than average. The signal is the drop in new lending, not defaults.
- MMCG (a feasibility consultancy) reports the same $676M and $272M figures (SECONDARY): https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/the-us-car-wash-industry-in-2026-the-gold-rush-meets-its-ceiling
- On screen, show this as a chart "Source: SBA 7(a) FOIA data, Net to Owner analysis", not as a document.

### DF6. California Water Code §10951 (AB 2230, 2012). PRIMARY, high confidence
Enrolled bill: https://www.leginfo.ca.gov/pub/11-12/bill/asm/ab_2201-2250/ab_2230_bill_20120827_enrolled.html
- "An in-bay car wash or a conveyor car wash permitted and constructed after January 1, 2014, shall do either of the following: (a) Install, use, and maintain a water recycling system that recycles and reuses at least 60 percent of the wash and rinse water. (b) Use recycled water provided by a water supplier for at least 60 percent of its wash and rinse water."
- Self-service washes are exempt; cities can require more (per the statute; seen in search snippet of the current code).

## 2. Oversupply and risk signals (2024–2026)

1. **~900 new sites a year for five years.** Zips court declaration, p. 3 and 23 (PRIMARY, sworn).
2. **New express openings fell from 943 (2022) to ~550 (2025)**, ~450 forecast for 2026. MMCG, Jul 4, 2026 (SECONDARY; underlying source not found). https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/the-us-car-wash-industry-in-2026-the-gold-rush-meets-its-ceiling
3. **"Market saturation is now the No. 1 operator concern."** NACS/ICA session coverage, May 15, 2026 (SECONDARY). Same piece: "M&A activity has cooled from peak levels"; some sites get "as much as 80% of revenue from memberships." https://www.convenience.org/stay-current/news/2026/may/15/ica-what’s-next-for-car-wash
4. **Mister's comps slowing:** +6.0% Q1 2025, +1.2% Q2, ~+3.1–3.4% Q3, +1.6% Q4. Full year +2.9% (PRIMARY, 8-Ks). Growth now comes from new sites and price, not more cars per site.
5. **Churn:** Zips' member churn "8%-12%" (monthly basis not stated; PRIMARY p. 20).
6. **Write-downs:** Driven Brands wrote off $1.18B on US car washes in 2023–2024, then sold for $385M (PRIMARY).
7. **Public-market verdict:** Mister IPO'd at $15 (June 2021, NYSE); taken private at $7.00 (May 19, 2026). Renaissance Capital: https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/83209/National-car-wash-brand-Mister-Car-Wash-prices-IPO-at-$15-low-end
8. **Lending pullback:** SBA car wash 7(a) dollars down 60% from FY2021 to FY2025 (PRIMARY, our calc).
9. **Cap rates up ~100 bp** from the 2022 peak (MMCG, SECONDARY). Mister's own sale-leasebacks: 6.6% (2024) and 5.9% (2025) implied.
10. **Promos:** $9.99 first-month unlimited (WOW Carwash, Links); Club Car Wash 50% off for a year for $0.01 (2026 press releases, SECONDARY). Not proof of a price war. https://www.newswire.com/news/for-1-penny-today-lock-in-50-off-any-membership-for-1-year-22688353
11. **Small-operator filings 2026:** My Car Wash LLC (M.D. Fla., Jan 15, 2026), Glacier Car and Dog Wash (D. Colo., May 4, 2026), Englewood Car Wash (M.D. Fla., Jun 2, 2026), all Chapter 11 (docket listings, SECONDARY). No large chain filed in 2026 that I found.

Counterpoints (keep it fair): SBA car wash loans default *less* than average; Mister grew members 7% in 2025 at 32.8% EBITDA margins; ICA survey says 91% of members plan to keep their plan.
